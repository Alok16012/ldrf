Two Missions. One Vision.

Empowerment & membership by LDRF — vocational training by RGLM

Legion Development and Research Foundation (LDRF)

Community Empowerment & Membership Drive
Registered under Section 8 with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India, LDRF builds grassroots volunteer networks, champions youth entrepreneurship and drives financial self-reliance initiatives like the Ani Earning Card.
  • CIN: U80903JK2017NPL009890
  • Village volunteers, block coordinators & tehsil heads
  • Digital facilitation & placement support

Rajiv Gandhi Literacy Mission (RGLM)

Registered Training Partner
Established in 2000 in Sopore, RGLM has decades of experience delivering certified technical, professional and creative skill training to empower the youth and women of Jammu & Kashmir.
  • Empower • Educate • Elevate — since 2000
  • Experienced trainers & hands-on learning
  • Certificate provided on course completion
